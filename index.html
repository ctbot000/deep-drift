    DEEPDRIFT

    Dig a hole. Get rich. Buy a bigger drill. Find out what is humming at 250 metres.

    No downloads, no dependencies. Arrow keys or WASD.

    How to play

    Controls

    • ◀ ▶ / A DMove, and drill sideways into rock
    • ▼ / SDrill straight down
    • ▲ / W / SpaceThrusters — the only way back up
    • EUse the building you are standing on
    • 1 2 3Beacon · Patch kit · Fuel cell
    • RCall a rescue tug when stranded
    • Esc · H · MPause · help · mute

    The loop

    Drill down, fill the hold with ore, fly back to the surface and sell it. Spend the money on a drill that bites harder rock, a tank that gets you deeper, and a hold that makes the trip worth it.

    Things that will kill you

    • Falling. Long drops into open caves hurt.
    • Magma. Bright orange, and it does not care about your hull.
    • Gas pockets. The green ones detonate when the tile next to them goes.
    • Running dry. No fuel means no thrusters, and the surface is a long way up.

    You cannot drill upwards — plan a way home before you spend the last of the tank.

    Paused

    Progress is saved automatically.

    THE CORE

    Two hundred and fifty metres down, the humming stops. You are the only person who has ever heard it.
